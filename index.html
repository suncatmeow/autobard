AUTOBARD · SUNCAT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how AutoBard by Suncat handles information when you use its locally packaged music app. Questions about privacy can be sent to suncat.data@gmail.com.

Music and information processed on your device

AutoBard generates compositions and synthesizes music on your device. The app does not require an account. Its music engine does not upload your songs, lyrics, imported audio, or listening preferences to a developer-operated server.

To provide its features, AutoBard may process and store the following information locally:

  • Compositions: notes, arrangements, instrument choices, lyrics, song settings, and edits.
  • Listening taste: your Like and Dislike votes, song identifiers, and numeric musical traits used to influence future suggestions. The saved taste record contains up to 128 recent votes and does not contain song audio or lyrics.
  • Selected files: audio, projects, favorites collections, or taste files that you choose to import, where file import is supported.
  • Session favorites: Serenade songs that you keep during the current open session. Downloading a favorites or project file preserves those songs beyond that session.

Project edits and listening taste can be saved in the app’s local WebView storage. Projects with imported audio require saving a project file to preserve that audio and the edits; they are not included in the ordinary project autosave.

Advertising, analytics, and permissions

The version described by this policy does not include advertising, analytics tracking, or automatic crash reporting to the developer. AutoBard does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

The Android app declares Internet permission. Core music generation uses the music engine packaged in the app and does not require sending your composition to an online generation service.

Android backup and device transfers

AutoBard allows Android’s app-data backup system. Depending on your device, Android version, backup provider, and settings, eligible local app data may be included in a cloud backup or transferred to another device. A restored backup may also restore previously saved app data. These operations are managed by Android and your backup provider; they are not uploads to a Suncat-operated server.

You can manage backup and restore options through your device and backup-provider settings.

Exports, sharing, and external links

If you choose to export or download a song, project, favorites collection, or taste file, the resulting file is saved to the destination supported by your device or browser. Exported projects can contain lyrics, musical settings, and imported audio. Only share files whose contents you are comfortable disclosing.

Where supported, the Share feature passes the exported audio file to the sharing service or app that you select. That service handles the file under its own privacy practices.

The app includes optional links to external research references and software credits. Opening an external website may disclose connection information, such as your IP address, to that website. External websites, operating-system services, backup providers, and app stores have their own privacy policies. This policy covers the AutoBard app, not a separately hosted website or those services.

Retention and deletion

Local project data remains until it is replaced, cleared, or removed with the app’s data. Listening taste retains up to 128 recent votes; older votes are retired when the limit is reached. You can remove saved taste votes using Reset taste.

Serenade’s session favorites are held in memory for the current session. Download them if you want to keep them after the app or session closes.

You can remove AutoBard’s local WebView data through Android’s app-storage controls. Clearing app data removes locally stored work and preferences, so export anything you wish to keep first. Downloaded files and files already shared with other services are separate copies and must be deleted from their respective destinations. Backups must be managed separately through your backup provider.

There is no AutoBard account to delete, and the developer cannot remotely access or delete the music stored only on your device.

Support email

If you contact suncat.data@gmail.com, the information you voluntarily send—such as your email address, message, and attachments—is processed to respond to you and troubleshoot your request. Email is handled through Gmail and is subject to Google’s privacy practices.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or meet applicable obligations. You can use the same address to ask for deletion of support correspondence. Avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

Data security

Local app data is stored using Android and WebView storage mechanisms. Protect your device and any exported files, and review the security settings of services you use for sharing and backup. AutoBard does not promise that local files are separately encrypted by the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when AutoBard’s features or data-handling practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
